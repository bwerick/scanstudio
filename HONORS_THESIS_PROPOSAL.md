# ScanStudio Honors Thesis Proposal and Timeline

## Project Summary

This honors thesis focuses on the continued development of ScanStudio, an open source system for digitizing books using affordable, everyday hardware. The goal of the project is to make it easier to convert book scanning videos into high quality PDF documents while preserving the original books. Unlike traditional digitization methods that often require cutting book bindings, ScanStudio uses video recordings to automatically identify clear page images, separate two page spreads into individual pages, and generate organized digital documents.

Beyond creating PDFs, the project also explores how modern AI tools can be used to better understand and organize digitized books. By producing clean, structured document collections, ScanStudio aims to support applications such as OCR, legal research, digital archives, and future AI systems that rely on high quality source material. Overall, this research focuses on making book digitization more accurate, scalable, and accessible while providing a strong foundation for future work in AI assisted document processing.
